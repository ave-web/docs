This page covers everything you need to know about company expenses — what qualifies, how much you can spend, and how to get reimbursed quickly.

## General Principles

Spend company money like your own. Before any purchase, ask yourself whether a reasonable colleague would consider this a legitimate business expense. Always get a receipt — no exceptions. If you're unsure whether something is reimbursable, ask your manager before you spend, not after.

## Expense Categories and Limits

| Category | Limit | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Travel (airfare, train) | Reasonable fare | Book economy; business class requires VP approval for flights over 6 hours |
| Hotels | $200/night (most cities), $250 in major metros | Book through the preferred travel tool where possible |
| Meals — solo | $50/day | Covers breakfast, lunch, and dinner combined |
| Meals — client entertainment | $100/person | Requires manager pre-approval before the event |
| Home office equipment | $500/year | Receipt required; one-off purchases over $200 need manager sign-off |
| Conferences & events | No set limit | Pre-approval required before registering |
| Software tools | See Tools & Stack page | Request via IT; do not expense personal subscriptions |

## How to Submit Expenses

<Steps>
  <Step title="Save all receipts">
    Capture a photo or PDF of every receipt at the time of purchase. Don't rely on your memory or your bank statement — itemised receipts are required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the expense tool">
    Log into Expensify (or the current expense tool — see the Tools & Stack page for the latest link) and start a new expense report.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new report">
    Give the report a clear name, such as "Q3 Customer Onsite — Chicago" or "October Travel." Group related expenses together.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add each expense">
    For every item, select the correct category, enter the amount and date, and write a short note explaining the business purpose. "Lunch" is not enough — write "Lunch with [Customer] during onsite visit."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach the receipt">
    Upload the receipt to each expense line. Expenses without receipts will be rejected at approval.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit for approval">
    Submit the report to your manager for approval. If you're unsure who your approval chain is, check with People Ops.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive reimbursement">
    Approved expenses are processed on the next payroll cycle. Check your pay stub to confirm the reimbursement was included.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Pre-Approval Requirements

Some expenses require you to get approval before you spend. Do not assume after-the-fact approval will be granted. Pre-approval is required for:

- Any single expense over $500
- Client entertainment (meals, events, or gifts)
- Team events and offsites
- All conference and event registrations
- International travel

## Common Mistakes

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Missing receipt">
    Expenses without receipts are rejected — no exceptions. If you lose a receipt, contact the vendor for a duplicate before submitting. Use the in-app photo capture immediately after each purchase so you never lose one.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="No business purpose">
    Every expense line must include a note that explains why the purchase was a legitimate business expense. "Dinner" or "Uber" isn't sufficient. Always name the customer, event, or project.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Personal items included">
    If a receipt includes both personal and business items, split them before submitting. Only submit the business portion. Submitting personal items as business expenses is a policy violation.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Note>
  Expenses older than 60 days may not be reimbursed. Submit promptly after each trip or purchase — don't let receipts pile up.
</Note>
