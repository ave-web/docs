The roadmap is our shared view of what we're building and why. This page explains how it's created, how priorities are set, and how you can contribute to shaping it.

## Planning Cadence

- **Annual strategy** — Set in Q4 for the following year. Establishes the big themes and bets we're committing to.
- **Quarterly planning** — A 6-week rolling cycle: discover → prioritize → plan. Each cycle produces a refreshed view of the next quarter.
- **Weekly sync** — The product team reviews progress, surfaces blockers, and adjusts priorities in response to new information.

## How Priorities Are Set

<Steps>
  <Step title="Gather input">
    Collect signals from customers, sales, support, and product analytics to build a clear picture of demand and pain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Score opportunities">
    Run each opportunity through an impact/effort matrix to compare them on a level playing field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Align with strategy">
    Check every candidate item against the company strategy and current OKRs. High impact + low effort + strong strategic fit rises to the top.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review large bets with leadership">
    Any initiative that requires significant investment or crosses team boundaries goes through a leadership review before it's committed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish and communicate">
    Finalized priorities are published in the roadmap tool and shared at the next All-Hands so everyone has the same picture.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How to Influence the Roadmap

<Steps>
  <Step title="Submit your idea">
    Add your idea to the ideas board *(link placeholder)* with as much context as you can — the problem, the customer impact, and any supporting data.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tag the right PM">
    Tag the product manager who owns the relevant area so it lands in the right queue and doesn't get lost.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Include evidence">
    Attach customer quotes, support ticket volume, usage data, or anything else that helps the PM understand the size of the problem.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the review cycle">
    PMs review the ideas board monthly and incorporate strong candidates into the next planning cycle. You'll hear back on what happened and why.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The roadmap reflects current thinking, not commitments. Priorities shift as we learn. That's a feature, not a bug.
</Note>
