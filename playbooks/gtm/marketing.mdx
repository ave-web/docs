This playbook covers how marketing works at the company — from campaign planning to brand guidelines to how we measure impact.

## How Marketing Plans

Marketing runs on a quarterly planning cycle that aligns with the product roadmap and the GTM calendar. At the start of each quarter, the marketing team reviews priorities with Sales and CS to align on pipeline targets, upcoming launches, and key customer moments. Plans are documented and shared in Notion before the quarter begins, and progress is reviewed in a monthly marketing sync.

## Content Creation Process

Follow this process for every piece of content, whether it's a blog post, a guide, or a webinar.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Brief">
    Define the audience, goal, channel, and key message before writing a word. A clear brief prevents rewrites. Use the content brief template in Notion.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Draft">
    Write the first version in Google Docs and share it with the brief attached. Keep it rough — the goal of a first draft is to have something to react to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review">
    Send to the relevant stakeholder for a content review, then pass to the copy editor for a line edit. Consolidate feedback before revising.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Design">
    If the piece needs visual assets, hand it to the design team with a clear brief on format, dimensions, and deadline. Allow at least 3 business days.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve">
    The content lead gives final sign-off before anything is scheduled. No content goes out without approval.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish & distribute">
    Schedule the content according to the channel calendar. Share in the relevant Slack channels and with any partner or customer audiences.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Measure">
    Check performance metrics at the 1-week and 1-month marks. Record findings in the content tracker and use them to inform future briefs.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Brand Guidelines

Our brand is one of our most important assets — consistency across every touchpoint builds trust and recognition. The full brand guidelines live in Figma (link placeholder). When creating any customer-facing material, follow these rules: always use the approved logo files from the brand kit, use the primary brand color `#ef7025` for key highlights and CTAs, and apply the approved font stack as defined in the Figma guidelines. If you're not sure whether something is on-brand, ask the design team before you publish.

<Warning>
  Don't create new brand assets without going through design. Rogue assets dilute the brand and create inconsistencies that are expensive to clean up.
</Warning>

## Requesting Marketing Support

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a request in Linear">
    Create a new issue under the `#marketing` project in Linear. Tag it with the appropriate campaign or content label.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Include the essentials">
    Every request must include a clear deadline, the target audience, the goal of the asset or campaign, and any existing assets or context that will help the team get started.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow enough lead time">
    Plan for 5 business days for standard requests such as one-pagers, social posts, or email copy. Allow at least 2 weeks for full campaigns. Requests without sufficient lead time may be deprioritised.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  All external content must be reviewed by a marketing team member before publishing. This includes blog posts, social media, and press — even if you drafted it yourself.
</Note>
