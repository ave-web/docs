## Mission

## Core Values

Our values define how we work — not just what we achieve, but how we achieve it. They show up in how we treat customers, how we give feedback, and how we make hard calls under pressure.

## How We Live Our Values

Values only matter if they show up in practice. You'll see our values reflected in our hiring rubrics — every interview loop includes a values assessment. They feed directly into performance reviews, where we evaluate not just what you delivered but how you delivered it. Day-to-day, they're the language we use to give and receive feedback.

When you're onboarding, reviewing your own work, or navigating a difficult situation, use the values as a gut-check. If an action doesn't feel consistent with who we say we are, it probably isn't.

For more on how values connect to your growth here, see the [Performance Review process](/people/performance) and our [Hiring Philosophy](/people/hiring).
