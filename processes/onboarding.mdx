This checklist covers everything you need to do in your first 30 days — from setting up your accounts to understanding how the team works. Take it one step at a time, and don't hesitate to ask questions along the way.

## Before Day 1

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign your offer letter">
    Sign your offer letter and send back all required HR forms by the deadline provided.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch for your welcome email">
    Expect a welcome email from IT with instructions for accessing your accounts and setting up your devices before your start date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install Slack">
    Download Slack and join the company workspace using the link in your welcome email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Block your calendar">
    Reserve your first morning for orientation. Block your calendar so nothing else gets scheduled over it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Week 1 Checklist

<Steps>
  <Step title="Complete IT setup">
    Get your laptop configured, activate all required accounts, and set up 1Password with the credentials IT provides.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Meet your manager and team">
    Attend your intro meetings, introduce yourself in the team Slack channel, and start building relationships with your direct colleagues.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read Mission & Values">
    Review the [Mission & Values](/company/mission-values) page to understand what drives the company and how decisions get made.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read How We Work">
    Read [How We Work](/how-we-work) to understand our norms around communication, collaboration, and working hours.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your tools">
    Follow the [Tools & Stack](/company/tools-stack) page to install and configure every tool you'll need for your role.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Join your Slack channels">
    Ask your manager which channels are relevant to your team and work. Join them and start listening.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Week 2–4 Checklist

- Shadow your team's key recurring meetings to understand how decisions are made and how work gets coordinated.
- Complete any mandatory compliance training assigned by People Ops.
- Schedule 1:1s with key cross-functional partners you'll work with regularly.
- Ship your first small task or project to get comfortable with the team's workflow and delivery process.
- Review your team's playbook so you understand how your specific team operates day to day.

## Key People to Meet

Schedule informal coffee chats with your manager, your manager's manager (skip-level), and at least two or three peers in your first two weeks. These conversations don't need an agenda — they're about building relationships and getting a feel for how people work. Your manager can help you identify the right people to start with.
