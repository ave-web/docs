## How to Request Time Off

Requesting time off is simple. Follow these steps to make sure your absence is covered and your team is informed.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check your team calendar">
    Review the shared team calendar to identify any conflicts with deadlines, launches, or teammates who are already out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give reasonable notice">
    For absences longer than 3 days, give at least 2 weeks' notice. For absences longer than 1 week, give at least 1 month's notice where possible.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit the request in the HR portal">
    Log in to the HR portal and submit your time-off request with the dates and type of leave. You'll receive a confirmation once it's approved.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Notify your manager in Slack">
    Send your manager a direct message in Slack to give them a heads-up, even after submitting the formal request. This keeps communication clear.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set an out-of-office in Gmail and Slack">
    Before your last day in the office, set an out-of-office auto-reply in Gmail and update your Slack status so teammates know when you'll be back.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Parental Leave

We support new parents with paid parental leave. Primary caregivers are eligible for up to 16 weeks of fully paid leave. Secondary caregivers are eligible for up to 8 weeks of fully paid leave. Leave can begin up to 2 weeks before the expected due date or adoption date, and does not need to be taken all at once.

<Note>
  Confirm these terms with People Ops before publishing. Parental leave policies may vary by country, state, or employment type. Reach out to People Ops to discuss your specific situation.
</Note>

## Other Leave Types

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Sick Leave">
    You don't need to use PTO when you're genuinely sick. Take the time you need to recover without counting it against your time-off balance. For extended illness lasting more than 5 consecutive days, notify People Ops so they can coordinate any additional support or medical leave options.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Bereavement Leave">
    If you experience the loss of an immediate family member, you're entitled to up to [X] days of paid bereavement leave. For extended family or close friends, speak with your manager and People Ops about additional flexibility. We'll support you through difficult times.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Jury Duty">
    If you're called for jury duty, notify your manager and People Ops as soon as you receive the summons. You'll receive your regular pay for the duration of jury service. Keep a copy of your jury duty paperwork for HR records.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Medical Leave (FMLA)">
    Employees who have been with the company for at least 12 months may be eligible for unpaid, job-protected leave under the Family and Medical Leave Act (FMLA) for qualifying serious health conditions, caregiving responsibilities, or the birth or placement of a child. Contact People Ops to understand your eligibility and initiate the process.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Blackout Periods

During high-intensity periods — such as quarter-end closes, major product launches, or critical fundraising windows — PTO requests may be restricted or require additional approval. Your manager will communicate blackout periods in advance whenever possible. If you've already booked travel during a period that later becomes a blackout window, talk to your manager as soon as possible to find a resolution.

<Warning>
  Unused PTO does not roll over at year end unless stated otherwise in your employment agreement. Check your contract or ask People Ops.
</Warning>
