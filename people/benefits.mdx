## Equity

We want everyone at the company to have a stake in what we're building. Full-time employees receive stock options as part of their compensation package. Options vest over a 4-year period with a 1-year cliff — meaning you vest 25% of your grant after your first year, then monthly thereafter.

To view your current grants, vesting schedule, and exercise options, log in to the equity management platform linked in the HR portal. If you have questions about your grant, tax implications, or how options work, reach out to People Ops or our CFO.

## Learning & Development

We invest in your growth. Every employee has access to an annual learning budget to spend on courses, books, conferences, and other professional development resources.

<Card title="Learning Budget" icon="graduation-cap">
  Each full-time employee receives a placeholder annual budget (e.g. $1,500/year) for learning and development. Use it for online courses, technical books, industry conferences, or certifications relevant to your role.

  To claim your budget, submit a request through the HR portal with a brief description of what you're purchasing and how it relates to your work. Reimbursements are processed within two pay cycles.
</Card>

## How to Enroll

Enrolling in benefits is straightforward. Follow these steps when you first join or during annual open enrollment.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to the HR portal">
    Navigate to the HR portal at [portal link placeholder] and sign in with your company credentials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Benefits">
    From the dashboard, select the **Benefits** tab to see all available plans and your current enrollment status.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your plan">
    Choose your preferred plans during the open enrollment window or within 30 days of your hire date. New hires can make elections outside of the standard enrollment period.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact People Ops with questions">
