---

If you're new here, start with this page. It covers the five most important things to read and do on your first day — enough to get you oriented, unblocked, and pointed in the right direction without overwhelming you with information all at once. By the end, you'll know where everything lives and how to find what you need.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Explore the structure">
    Company Brain is organised into two main sections. **Home** covers everything company-wide: who we are, how we work, our processes, and our people. **Playbooks** contains team-specific guides for Engineering, Product & Design, Go-to-Market, and Finance & Legal. Start at the top of the sidebar and scan the headings — you don't need to read everything now, just get a feel for what's here.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read Mission & Values">
    Head to [Mission & Values](/company/mission-values) and read it in full. Understanding why we exist and what we care about will give you the context to interpret everything else you read in Company Brain. It's short, and it matters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow the Onboarding Checklist">
    The [Onboarding Checklist](/processes/onboarding) is your step-by-step guide to your first week. It walks you through every account to set up, every person to schedule a coffee chat with, and every tool to configure. Work through it in order and check items off as you go.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find your team's playbook">
    Navigate to the **Playbooks** tab in the sidebar and open the section for your function. Each team maintains its own playbook covering day-to-day workflows, tools, rituals, and escalation paths. Your team lead can point you to the most important pages to read first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bookmark this site">
    Add Company Brain to your browser bookmarks and pin it in your Slack or Teams workspace. You'll be coming back here often — make it easy to reach. If your team has a shared bookmarks channel, drop the link there too.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If you can't find something, use the search bar at the top. If it still doesn't exist, add it!
</Note>
